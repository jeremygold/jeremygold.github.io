## Summary

Using an old "If you can fix it you can keep it" R0DE microphone, and some random bits of brass and aluminium I made this suspended microphone stand for online meetings. Interesting using a mini gas torch and solder to join the brass rods.

Heavy inspiration from DIY Perks: [Building a quality USB-C microphone](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LoQu3XXIayc&t=23s)
