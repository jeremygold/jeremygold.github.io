## Summary

So I asked myself one day, what would a Yin Yang symbol look like if it was three dimensional, and how would I build it...

This is what I've come up with - A carefully counterbalanced 3D printed spherical half yin yang, mounted to a motor that spins at about 1800 RPM, and red and blue LED panel strobes either side.

Raspberry PI pico controls the strobe timing to generate different effects...
