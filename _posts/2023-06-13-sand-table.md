## Summary

I stumbled across some similar ideas on the internet, and realized that I had most of the bits I needed to get started on this in my parts boxes.

I had just built a standing work desk in the Mad Inventing room, and decided it would be great to build a robot controlled magnetic sand table into it.

![Sandtable Still](assets/img/sandtable-still.jpg)

Onshape design is at [Sand Table](https://cad.onshape.com/documents/89b24d632392923c29827ebf/w/f01a8f980987ce535a15ee7e/e/f1cf3e2f8ec18e58d7f0d0ca)
